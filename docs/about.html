About Me

Creator of LiveSticky and a passionate builder.

Hey everyone, I'm u/iammesutkaya (Mesut Kaya). You might have seen some of my comics floating around Reddit! By day I'm a UI/UX designer, but in my free time, I love to draw and stream.

When I started streaming, I really wanted a tool for my own subreddit that could automatically pin a live post when I go live and keep a dashboard updated with my current status. I built LiveSticky just for myself initially, but after realizing there wasn't really anything similar out there for the rest of Reddit, I decided to polish it up and make it public for other creators to use.
