Core features

Automatic live posts

Pins a fresh post the second you go live — on any platform.

Checks Twitch, YouTube, and Kick every 2 minutes. The moment you go live it submits the post, applies your custom flair, sets the suggested sort to New so comments behave like live chat, and pins it — no manual action needed.

Real-time stats

Viewer count, category, and uptime stay current — no refresh.

The live post body, the interactive dashboard, and your sidebar widget all update through Reddit Realtime as your numbers change — viewers see the latest without reloading.

Multistream aware

Tracks Twitch, YouTube, and Kick together in one dashboard.

Broadcasting to several platforms at once? The dashboard lists each live stream with its own thumbnail, viewer count, and one-tap watch button, so your community picks where to watch.

Clean archive when you wrap

Grace period, VOD archive, and a top-clips highlights post.

Waits 6 minutes first, so a brief OBS restart or platform hiccup won't spam duplicate posts. Then it edits the post into an offline archive with VOD links, locks and unpins it, and compiles your top 5 Twitch clips into a standalone highlights post.

Sidebar widget

A live STREAM STATUS widget in your community sidebar.

Keeps a "STREAM STATUS" sidebar widget updated automatically with live/offline state, category, viewers, and uptime — right alongside the pinned post.

Built for mods

Pinned mod comment, one-click reset, and rate-limit safety.

Auto-pins a customizable moderator comment (Discord links, rules, socials), gives you a one-click status reset right in mod tools, and uses Redis caching to stay fast and safely within platform API limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LiveSticky really free?

Yes. LiveSticky is 100% free and open-source. Because it runs on the Reddit Developer Platform, Reddit handles all the hosting and infrastructure automatically.

Do I need coding experience or a server?

Not at all! You don't need a server, and you don't need to touch any code. You just click "Install" on the Reddit Developer Portal, enter your Twitch/YouTube/Kick usernames in your Mod Tools, and you're done.

What if I stream to multiple platforms at the same time?

LiveSticky handles multistreaming perfectly. The dashboard will automatically update to show every platform you are currently live on, displaying them side-by-side so your viewers can choose their preferred player.

Will this spam my subreddit if my stream disconnects?

No. LiveSticky has a built-in 6-minute grace period. If your OBS crashes or your internet blips, the post stays live. It only converts into an offline VOD archive after you've been offline for 6 straight minutes.
