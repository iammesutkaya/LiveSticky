Mod Menu & Tools

How to manage LiveSticky directly from your subreddit using built-in mod commands.

Moderator Menu Items

LiveSticky registers custom moderator commands that you can run directly from your community's Mod Menu feed:

Menu ItemDescription & Action
Refresh LiveSticky Clears cached data, re-fetches profile images, updates the dashboard, and runs an immediate status check. Ideal for validating new settings or force-syncing channel changes immediately.

How to Access Mod Tools

To run any of the actions above, ensure you are logged in with moderator permissions on your subreddit:

  1. 1
    Navigate to your subreddit's home feed (e.g. r/yourcommunity) in the official Reddit mobile app or desktop browser.
  2. 2
    Click the Mod Tools shield icon or the overflow menu (three dots button ...) near the top of the feed page.
  3. 3
    Scroll to the Apps or Mod Commands submenu, and click your desired action. The app will immediately trigger the action and show a confirmation toast notification.
