v1.1.317

Released SEP 12, 2026

You now get told when a post could not be pinned

  • A modmail when both slots are full: If LiveSticky creates a post but both of your community's pinned slots are already taken, it now sends your mod team a message explaining what happened and what to do. Previously this failed silently and the only clue was a live thread sitting in new instead of at the top.
  • It fixes itself once you free a slot: Unpin any post and LiveSticky claims the free slot within 2 minutes, with nothing else to do.
  • Honest logs: LiveSticky used to report "posted and pinned" even when the post got no slot. It now says which of the two actually happened.

To be clear about what was never at risk: the post is always created. Pinning has always been a separate, best-effort step, so a full slot has never meant a missing live thread.

Technical details & developer notes

Added

  • ModMail alert on pin failure: alertPinFailure() fires from the one place that knows the outcome, the post-sticky verification inside pinPostWithFallback, so it covers the live, offline, clips and dashboard posts alike. One shared cooldown key (modmail_cooldown_pin) means a subreddit with both slots taken gets one message rather than four. Uses the delivered/failed cooldown shape already used for settings problems: 24h after a successful send, 5 minutes after a failed one, so a transient ModMail outage is not silently muted for a day.

Fixed

  • pinPostWithFallback now reports its result: it returned void and swallowed its own failures, so the create path logged Successfully posted and pinned unconditionally - including when the post held no slot. It returns Promise<boolean> and the caller logs what actually happened. That false log line is part of why an unpinned live thread was hard to diagnose from the outside.

v1.1.316

Released SEP 12, 2026

The live thread now gets a pinned slot, and LiveSticky reliably knows its own posts

  • Your live thread gets pinned, even in a busy community: A subreddit has only two pinned slots. The Top Clips post used to claim one when a stream ended and never give it back, so with one of your own threads in the other slot, the live thread had nowhere to go and was quietly left unpinned. The clips post now releases its slot when the stream starts and takes it back when the stream ends.
  • Rename your posts freely: LiveSticky now recognises its own posts by an invisible tag in the post body instead of by words in the title. Custom titles for your live thread, offline post and clips post are fully supported, and renaming one can no longer confuse the bot.
  • No more mixing up posts: The live thread, offline post, Top Clips post and monthly Top 20 post can never be mistaken for one another, so none of them can be edited or pinned in another's place. This closes the last route to the problem fixed in v1.1.314.
  • Clearer pinning: When both slots really are occupied by other posts, LiveSticky now says so plainly in its logs instead of failing quietly.

Version 1.1.315 was superseded during upload and never reached review, so this release carries its changes.

Technical details & developer notes

Fixed

  • Sticky slot released at go-live: with stickyHighlightsPost enabled, postStreamHighlights claimed a legacy slot at stream end and nothing ever released it, so it held one of two slots across every following stream. The LIVE branch now unstickies highlights_post_id alongside the existing offline_post_id unsticky, before the live post calls pinPostWithFallback.
  • Recovery identifies posts by marker, not by id list: findExistingSubredditPost matched a hardcoded title keyword and excluded a list of known post ids. Neither is sound - livePostTitle is a mod setting, and the monthly top-20 post's id is never written to Redis so no exclude list can reach it. Every managed body now carries an empty-label link marker ([](#ls-live), [](#ls-offline), [](#ls-clips), [](#ls-monthly)), stamped at the four body-build sites. The live lookup matches the marker alone; the recycled offline and clips lookups accept marker OR legacy title keyword so posts from older versions keep working until an edit stamps them. Verified on r/live_sticky_dev that Reddit preserves the marker across both submit and edit round-trips.

Removed

  • Community Highlights fallback: pinPostWithFallback called AddPostToHighlights when no legacy slot was available. Measured directly on the Devvit runtime, both that method and GetIsPostHighlighted return 12 UNIMPLEMENTED, and the branch had never executed in production logs. Removed along with the LinksAndCommentsDefinition plugin handle and the HighlightedPostLabel import. verifyAndRepinIfNeeded simplifies to a stickied check. Apps get the two legacy slots; highlights slots 3-6 are not addressable, and Post.sticky() positions 3 and 4 are profile pins.

v1.1.314

Released SEP 11, 2026

Fixes a clips post being pinned in place of the live thread

  • The right post gets pinned when you go live: On communities using a custom live post title, LiveSticky could pin the previous stream's Top Clips post instead of creating a new live thread, overwriting that post's body with live stream stats while its title still read "Top Clips". It now creates the live thread as expected.
  • Custom live post titles are fully supported: Reconnecting to an existing live thread no longer depends on your title containing the words "is LIVE", so renaming the thread in your settings is safe.
  • Posts LiveSticky manages stay separate: The live thread, offline post, Top Clips post and dashboard post can no longer be mistaken for one another during recovery, so none of them will be edited or pinned in another's place.
  • Yesterday's thread stays closed: Recovery only considers posts created during the current stream, so a concluded thread from an earlier stream is never re-pinned.

Communities on the default live post title were unaffected. If yours uses a custom title and Top Clips posts are enabled, this release fixes it.

Technical details & developer notes

Fixed

  • Search fallback adopted the wrong post: the offline path deletes live_post_id, so every go-live runs findExistingSubredditPost. It was called with the hardcoded keyword 'is LIVE' while livePostTitle is a mod setting, so the listing pass (which requires the title to contain the keyword) missed on customized titles. The searchPosts fallback then filtered results with an author check alone and no title check. Reddit search is fuzzy and the default highlights footer contains the word "live", so the first app-authored hit was the previous stream's Top Clips post. It was adopted as the live thread, passed through post.edit({ text }) - which rewrites the body but never the title - and pinned. The fallback now applies the same predicate as the listing pass.
  • Live recovery no longer matches on title: queryKeyword accepts null, and the helper takes notBefore and excludeIds. The live lookup passes null, bounds candidates to the current stream's started_at, and excludes dashboard_post_id, offline_post_id, highlights_post_id and last_highlights_post_id. If started_at is unavailable the lookup is skipped and a fresh post is created.
  • Cross-adoption between managed posts: the 'is offline' and 'Top Clips' lookups keep their keywords and gained the same exclusion set, so no recovery path can reach a post another path owns.

Added

  • src/post-recovery.ts: the candidate predicate extracted as a pure isRecoveryCandidate(), testable without the Devvit runtime. Test count 99 → 105.

v1.1.313

Released AUG 26, 2026

Everything since v1.1.285: safer post handling, restored features, and accessibility

  • Safer post recovery: When LiveSticky reconnects to a thread after a restart, it now only ever adopts posts it created itself. Previously it could match another post in your community and clear the comments on it.
  • No duplicate live threads: If LiveSticky loses track of its own thread mid-stream, such as after an app update, it now reconnects to the existing one instead of pinning a second live thread beside it.
  • Monthly Top 20 clips fixed: The monthly clip compilation now publishes on schedule. It had been failing silently.
  • Create Dashboard is back in Mod Tools: The action to create the interactive dashboard post had gone missing from the mod menu.
  • Your timezone setting now applies: The Streamer Timezone option was quietly ignored and every date fell back to UTC. Dates in your post titles and bodies now use the timezone you picked.
  • Safer clip formatting: Unusual characters in Twitch clip titles can no longer break post layout or slip an unexpected link into a pinned thread.
  • Cleaner offline threads: Templates that use live stream stats no longer leave raw placeholders like {stream_game} behind once the stream ends.
  • YouTube quota protection: If YouTube's daily API limit is reached, LiveSticky pauses those requests and resumes automatically at midnight Pacific.
  • More reliable Refresh: Refresh LiveSticky now clears rate-limit blocks for immediate retesting, and no longer leaves the dashboard blank when it runs alongside a scheduled check.
  • Lighter background activity: Wiki archive pages are only rewritten when their content actually changes, instead of on every check.
  • Keyboard and motion support: The dashboard is now fully keyboard navigable with visible focus rings, and honours your system's reduced-motion setting.

This release rolls up versions 1.1.286 through 1.1.312, which were superseded during review and never became available to install.

Technical details & developer notes

Fixed

  • Select settings read as arrays: Devvit returns select settings as ['UTC'], not 'UTC'. All three streamerTimezone read sites called .trim() on the raw value. Two were masked by surrounding try/catch (silently falling back to UTC); a third, in the settings validator, threw on every scheduler tick. Added settingText() as the single accessor and widened the validator's input types to unknown.
  • Self-healing search scoped to the app account (B3): findExistingSubredditPost ran an unfiltered searchPosts and adopted posts[0]. ensureStickyOfflinePost then removed every comment on that post before the edit() that would have failed. Results are now filtered by getAppUser().
  • Self-healing read from the listing index: findExistingSubredditPost relied on searchPosts, whose index lags by minutes, so a live post created earlier in the same stream was invisible to recovery. Now tries getPostsByUser on the app account first, with search kept as a deeper-history fallback.
  • Monthly highlights ReferenceError (B1): runMonthlyHighlights referenced an undeclared wikiArchive, throwing into a catch block on every run.
  • Dashboard menu route (B2): createDashboardPost had no endpoint and no manifest entry; both restored.
  • Clip title escaping (B6): escapeMarkdownBrackets() and escapeHtmlEntities() applied to clip titles, creators and URLs on both the markdown and wiki render paths.
  • Offline template variables (B5): the isLive === false branch of replaceTemplateVariables skipped game, viewers and uptime.
  • Dashboard pointer garbage collected (B7): dashboard_post_id removed from the 7-day wipe list.
  • Token invalidation (B8): Twitch and Kick cached tokens are dropped on HTTP 401/403 instead of being replayed until TTL.
  • Scheduler overlap (M1): SET NX lock with ownership read-back and a 5-minute TTL; the manual refresh path waits for an in-flight run rather than being dropped.

Added

  • Wiki write fingerprinting (B4): FNV-1a content hash in Redis with a 1h TTL, checked before any API call. Steady-state wiki traffic per tick went from ~30 calls to zero.
  • Settings validation (M10): grace period, publish time, avatar host and timezone are validated each tick and reported through ModMail, rate-limited to once a day with a short retry window after a failed send.
  • HTTP timeouts (M9): every upstream call routed through fetchWithTimeout (10s).
  • Pre-ship gates (M2): tsc --noEmit and the Vitest suite run in both release.sh and upload.sh before anything is uploaded. Test count 59 → 99.
  • Accessibility (U1-U6): token-based :focus-visible rings, keyboard-operable thread chip, roving-tabindex tablist with arrow keys, modal focus trap and aria-modal, and a prefers-reduced-motion block.

v1.1.244

Released AUG 13, 2026

Stream Highlights Upgrade, Wiki Archive & Monthly Top 20 Post

  • Monthly Top 20 Clips Post: Automatically publish a monthly recap of your subreddit's top 20 Twitch clips with customizable scheduling (Start, Middle, or End of month).
  • Flexible Stream Clip Posts: Choose between publishing a new clip post per stream session or recycling a single master sticky clips post.
  • Permanent Wiki Archives: Store full clip histories on your subreddit wiki pages with automatic links from clip posts.
Technical details & developer notes

Added

  • Previous Stream Clip Link: Automatically links to the previous stream's clip post at the bottom of the body (available as {previous_highlights_url} template placeholder).
  • Reuse Single Highlights Post setting (opt-in): Recycles a single permanent master clips post after each stream.
  • Wiki Clip Archive (opt-in): Keeps full clip history on subreddit wiki pages for stream clips and monthly top-20.
  • Monthly Top 20 Clips Post: Publishes the 20 most-watched Twitch clips from the previous calendar month.
  • Sticky Highlights Post setting: Keeps the clip compilation pinned, re-pinning it after each stream if it slips.

Changed

  • Stream highlights post defaults to creating a new post per stream session, allowing per-stream variables like {date} and {stream_title} in post titles.

Fixed

  • Fixed YouTube livestream detection on dashboard.
  • The {date} variable now respects the configured Streamer Timezone instead of always using UTC.
